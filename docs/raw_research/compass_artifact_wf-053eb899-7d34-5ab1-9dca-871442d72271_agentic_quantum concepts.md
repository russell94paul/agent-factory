# Can CELL//OS Produce Groundbreaking AI Research? A Literature Map and Ranked Contribution Plan (2024–September 2026)

## TL;DR
- **Yes, there is a real, defensible publication in here — but not where the branding points.** The single most open and valuable contribution is a **finance-specific fusion that nobody has shipped as of September 2026: a recursive self-improvement loop whose acceptance gate for each self-modification is a *multiple-testing-corrected financial statistic* (Deflated Sharpe / PBO / SPA) evaluated under a structurally sealed, leakage-controlled, delayed real-world utility.** The abstract idea "acceptance gate = multiple-testing control" was already published in 2026 for *general* agents (PACE, SEA), and DSR/PBO was applied *post-hoc* to LLM trading search (Gençay 2026), but the two have not been joined into a recursive commit gate for trading — that white space is the "Deflated Gödel Machine."
- **Rank order to pursue:** (1) **Co-evolved risk/overfitting red-team evaluator anchored to live-vs-backtest divergence** (highest fit + most defensible, novelty ~7/10); (2) **Deflated Gödel Machine** (highest novelty on the finance fusion, ~6–7/10, but statistically hard and partly pre-empted at the abstract level); (3) **Organizational-topology evolution with information barriers and safety vetoes as first-class search dimensions** (most crowded — ADAS/MaAS/GPTSwarm/AFlow already own the core — but constraint-aware org search is genuinely open, novelty ~5/10).
- **Two side verdicts:** "Agent decoherence" (drift monitoring) is a **real, barely-populated niche** worth a workshop paper but not a flagship claim; **quantum-cognition for agents is a metaphor only** — there is no substantive empirical line of work applying quantum decision theory to LLM multi-agent systems, and framing it as physics will hurt credibility.

## Key Findings

1. **The self-improving-agent field is moving fast and is now dominated by the Clune/Schmidhuber/Cambridge-NVIDIA lineage.** Darwin Gödel Machine (DGM), Huxley-Gödel Machine (HGM), HyperAgents, and Red Queen Gödel Machine (RQGM) define the frontier. Almost all of them optimize against **static coding benchmarks** (SWE-bench, Polyglot). None of them use a **real-money or delayed real-world financial utility**, and none use **formal multiple-comparisons control** as an acceptance gate.

2. **RQGM already occupies "co-evolving learned evaluators under non-stationary utility."** This means candidate #3 (co-evolved risk red-team) must be positioned as a *domain instantiation with a non-hackable ground-truth anchor* (live-vs-backtest divergence), not as a new co-evolution mechanism — otherwise it is derivative of RQGM.

3. **The multi-agent topology search space is heavily populated** (ADAS, AFlow, MaAS/agentic supernet, GPTSwarm, AgentPrune, AgentDropout, DyLAN, MAS-GPT, AgentSquare, MASS). What remains genuinely open is **constraint-aware organizational search** — treating information barriers, veto rights, and fail-closed safety gates as first-class, *rewarded* search dimensions with org-level (clade-level) credit assignment.

4. **Agent-driven quant research is real but the overfitting rigor is weak and live results are scarce/suspect.** RD-Agent(Q), AlphaAgent, STAR/STAR Analyst, AQuA, QuantAgent, TradingAgents all exist; most report backtests, and the few "live" numbers (QuantAgents' 111.87% A-stock return) are implausibly high and should be treated as marketing until independently replicated. Crucially, **none of the mainstream agentic quant systems use Deflated Sharpe / PBO / CPCV as an in-loop gate** — a documented gap CELL//OS's DEFLATOR/CPCV modules are designed to fill.

5. **A 2026 finance paper (Gençay, "What survives honest evaluation?") demonstrates the killer caveat for candidate #1:** a planted look-ahead oracle "survives Deflated Sharpe and probability-of-backtest-overfitting testing completely" (DSR = 1.00). **This proves DSR/PBO alone cannot catch data leakage** — statistical deflation and structural sealing are complementary, not substitutes. This is itself a publishable, decision-relevant insight and the strongest argument for CELL//OS's combined WARDEN+DEFLATOR+research-firewall design.

### B. The Three Candidate Contributions

#### Candidate 1 — "Deflated Gödel Machine"
**The claim:** recursive self-improvement / archive search whose acceptance criterion for self-modifications is a multiple-testing-corrected statistic (Deflated Sharpe, PBO, SPA/Reality Check, minimum track record length) against a pre-registered backtest budget, under a real, non-hackable-but-noisy, delayed, non-stationary utility (live/shadow futures PnL under prop-firm rules).

**What is already taken:**
- The *abstract* idea "committing a self-modification is uncontrolled adaptive multiple testing, so gate it with a formal statistical test" is **already published for general agents**: **PACE** (arXiv 2606.08106) recasts committing as a sequential hypothesis test with an anytime-valid e-process controlling false-commit probability at α; **SEA** (arXiv 2607.00871) admits self-edits only through an FWER-budgeted, peek-safe gate with a LORD-family FDR ledger. This *substantially reduces* the novelty of the generic framing.
- The *finance* half — applying DSR/PBO to an LLM-driven strategy search with a recorded trial ledger — is **already published post-hoc**: **Gençay** (arXiv 2608.27734) deflates all reported performance by the search's own trial count and computes PBO via CSCV.
- **AQuA** (arXiv 2608.12841) is the nearest neighbor: recursive research-process self-improvement on sealed trading backtests — but it *deliberately chose structural sealing over statistical multiple-testing control* and reports no DSR/PBO/CPCV.

**What remains open (the defensible wedge):** No published system fuses (a) *recursive self-improvement / evolutionary archive*, (b) a *multiple-testing-corrected financial acceptance gate* (DSR/PBO/SPA over a pre-registered backtest budget) as the *in-loop commit rule* (not post-hoc certification), and (c) a *delayed, non-stationary real-world utility* (live/shadow futures PnL under prop-firm trailing-drawdown rules). This exact triple is white space.

**The killer defensibility insight:** Gençay's planted look-ahead oracle "survives Deflated Sharpe and probability-of-backtest-overfitting testing completely" (DSR = 1.00). Therefore the *contribution is not DSR-as-gate alone* — it is the **combination** of a statistical gate (DEFLATOR) with structural leakage sealing (research firewall + WARDEN + CPCV) and a delayed live anchor. Framing the paper as "why deflation is necessary but not sufficient, and what the missing pieces are" is both novel and honest.

**Minimal rigorous experiment:** (i) Build the archive over strategy-generating Operatives; (ii) pre-register a backtest budget N (the DEFLATOR trial count); (iii) accept a self-modification only if its purged/CPCV out-of-sample Deflated Sharpe exceeds threshold given N *and* PBO < 0.5 *and* it survives a shadow/live embargoed window; (iv) baselines: raw "keep if Sharpe up" (DGM-style), held-out gate (RSEA-style), PACE e-process gate, and post-hoc DSR (Gençay-style); (v) metric: out-of-sample/live Deflated Sharpe of accepted strategies and false-commit rate; (vi) run a planted-leakage oracle as a red-team control to show the gate's blind spots.

**Cost/timeline:** The statistical machinery is cheap; the binding constraint is the **delayed live/shadow anchor**. A credible epoch structure is 6–12 months of shadow trading, structured as RQGM-style epochs (e.g., monthly evaluator-frozen epochs) so per-epoch guarantees hold while the utility drifts. Token/compute cost per DGM/HGM-class run is dominated by evaluation, not generation; expect low-thousands of USD in LLM calls plus market-data infrastructure.

**Biggest risks:** statistical (DSR assumptions on non-normal, autocorrelated futures returns; multiple-testing bookkeeping across a growing archive); engineering (shadow-trading fidelity, slippage/latency); reward hacking (the agent learning to satisfy the deflated statistic without real edge); data leakage (survives DSR — see above). **Novelty: 6–7/10** — high on the finance fusion and the necessity-not-sufficiency result; capped because PACE/SEA/Gençay already established the constituent halves in 2026.

#### Candidate 2 — "Organizational Evolution" (topology as the search space)
**What is already taken:** the core idea of searching over multi-agent topology/roles/communication is thoroughly covered — ADAS (search over agentic system code), AFlow (MCTS over workflow graphs), MaAS (probabilistic agentic supernet, query-adaptive), GPTSwarm (optimizable communication graphs), AgentPrune/AgentDropout (graph sparsification), DyLAN (dynamic team selection), MAS-GPT (one-shot MAS generation), AgentSquare (modular search), MASS (prompts + topology). 2026 follow-ups (e.g., "Evolve as a Team," organizational-science-of-MAS work) extend to team-level self-evolution and decoupling "who/how/which algorithm."

**What remains open:** none of these treat **information barriers (research firewalls), veto rights, and fail-closed safety gates as first-class, rewarded search dimensions**, and none do **org-level / clade-level credit assignment under those constraints**. Constraint-aware organizational search — where the search must *preserve* an information barrier between hypothesis-generating and evaluating Operatives while optimizing lineage outcomes — is genuinely novel and maps directly onto CELL//OS's Cell-Mesh topologies and research firewall.

**Minimal experiment:** define a topology search space including barrier/veto edges; score by clade-level (HGM CMP-style) outcomes; baselines: GPTSwarm/MaAS/AFlow without constraints; ablate the barrier to show it is not merely a cost. Metric: performance *and* a leakage/contamination measure that the barrier is supposed to protect.

**Risks:** hard to show the constraint *helps* rather than just constrains; credit assignment across org levels is noisy. **Novelty: 5/10** — crowded core, but the constraint-as-search-dimension framing is defensible.

#### Candidate 3 — "Co-evolved risk / red-team evaluator"
**What is already taken:** RQGM (arXiv 2606.26294) already co-evolves task agents with learned evaluators under non-stationary utility, including **adversarial regularization of over-lenient evaluators** — exactly the mechanism a "harsh red-team judge" would use. AgenticRed (arXiv 2601.13518) optimizes agentic systems for automated red-teaming. So the *co-evolution mechanism* is taken.

**What remains open (the wedge):** RQGM's stated core limitation is that "the evaluator is only as good as its anchor." A finance red-team evaluator **anchored to live-vs-backtest divergence as non-hackable ground truth** resolves exactly that limitation in a domain where the anchor is objective and delayed. This is the most *defensible* of the three because it (a) plugs a named RQGM limitation, (b) has an objective anchor that resists LLM-judge bias, and (c) fits CELL//OS's THE CRUCIBLE + JUDGE + DISTANCE modules natively.

**Minimal experiment:** co-evolve a "PBO/overfit red-team" Operative whose reward is accuracy at predicting live-vs-backtest divergence; baseline against a frozen DSR/PBO evaluator and a generic LLM judge; measure calibration of the red-team's overfit predictions on out-of-sample live data. **Novelty: 7/10** — highest fit + defensibility; the anchor is the innovation.

### C. Additional / Possibly Better Contributions the Literature Suggests
1. **"Deflation ≠ leakage control" as a standalone result** (extending Gençay's oracle finding into a general theorem about self-improving research agents): statistical multiple-testing gates and structural sealing are provably complementary. This is cheaper than a full live deployment and highly citable.
2. **A benchmark/environment for self-improving agents under delayed, non-stationary, non-hackable utility** — the field (DGM/HGM/RQGM) is starved of non-saturating, non-hackable benchmarks (an explicit RQGM limitation). A prop-firm-rules shadow-trading environment (PROPSIM + DISTANCE) could become the "SWE-bench of delayed-utility self-improvement."
3. **Clade-level (CMP) credit assignment applied to organizational topologies** — combining HGM's metaproductivity with constraint-aware org search.

### D. Prior Art in Agent-Driven Quant Research — Honest Assessment
- **RD-Agent(Q)** (Microsoft, NeurIPS 2025): strongest engineering; real-market backtests via Qlib; ~2× annualized return vs classical factor libraries with 70% fewer factors; **no DSR/PBO/CPCV gate**; no live results; authors explicitly caution outputs are not investment advice.
- **AlphaAgent** (KDD '25): decay-resistance via AST-originality + complexity control; backtests 2021–2024 on CSI500/S&P500 (~11%/8.74% excess, IR 1.5/1.05 after costs); **no DSR/PBO/CPCV**; no live.
- **STAR / STAR Analyst** (SSRN, 2026; overlaps RQGM authorship): evolves the *researcher*; leakage-controlled forward protocol on CSI300 2026Q1; host-side evaluator + isolated data; closest to CELL//OS's research-firewall philosophy; still single-market.
- **AQuA** (2026): recursive research-process improvement; **structural sealing (sealed sandbox, causal-by-construction DSL, split-metric selection, walk-forward, embargo)**; backtest-only (crypto + US equities); **explicitly no DSR/PBO**; no live; cost not reported.
- **QuantAgent / TradingAgents / QuantAgents**: role-based LLM trading firms; QuantAgents claims live 111.87% A-stock return (Sharpe 2.02) Q3'24–Q1'25 — treat as unverified/marketing; HuggingFace commenters called TradingAgents' numbers "too optimistic to be true." The lifelong real-time benchmark "When Agents Trade" (Qian et al., arXiv 2510.11695, ACM Web Conference 2026) introduces the Agent Market Arena; the companion StockBench (arXiv 2510.02209) states directly that "most LLM agents struggle to outperform the simple buy-and-hold baseline."
- **Bottom line:** overfitting rigor across the field is weak-to-absent, and credible live results are essentially nonexistent. CELL//OS's DEFLATOR/PROPSIM/CPCV/WARDEN stack is a genuine differentiator.

### E. Agent Drift / "Decoherence" — Verdict
A small but real 2026 literature exists: "Agent Drift" (arXiv 2601.04170) formalizes behavioral degradation in multi-agent LLM systems and proposes an Agent Stability Index and mitigations (episodic memory consolidation, drift-aware routing, adaptive anchoring); "How Fast Do Agents Rot?" (arXiv 2609.01660) shows task success follows a geometric per-step reliability law that saturates below 1, guaranteeing eventual collapse at long horizons; "Beyond pass@1" (arXiv 2603.29231) proposes reliability-decay metrics. **Verdict:** this is a real, thinly-populated niche — a solid workshop paper if CELL//OS's JUDGE contributes a *formal* drift/decoherence metric with a live financial ground truth, but not a flagship "groundbreaking" claim on its own. Beyond generic MLOps drift detection, formal treatment is nascent.

### F. Quantum-Cognition for Agents — Verdict
Busemeyer & Bruza's *Quantum Models of Cognition and Decision* (Cambridge) is a serious, established program in **human** cognitive science, and Busemeyer/Bruza themselves stress it is a **formal analogy, not a claim about quantum brains** ("agnostic toward the 'quantum mind' hypothesis"). Applications to AI agents are sparse and mostly position pieces (e.g., arXiv 1808.04600 proposing quantum-probabilistic modeling of agent decisions). **Verdict: metaphor only.** The "quantum-principled Operative" (belief superpositions, deferred commitment, entanglement vs. barriers, decoherence-as-drift) is a useful *design vocabulary* and can be cited to real quantum-decision-theory literature, but there is no substantive empirical line applying it to LLM multi-agent systems. **Publishing it as physics would damage credibility; publish it as a design metaphor with honest framing.**

### G. Open-Endedness / Quality-Diversity for Strategy Discovery
MAP-Elites and QD (Mouret/Clune lineage) are mature; the first application of MAP-Elites to trade execution appeared in 2026 (arXiv 2601.22113), generating regime-specialist strategies indexed by liquidity/volatility, with 8–10% in-niche gains but generalization problems. **Implication:** QD is a natural fit for CELL//OS's ARENA (regime-indexed strategy archive under non-stationarity), and is under-explored in finance — a credible secondary contribution, but generalization/overfitting under non-stationarity is the open problem.

## Recommendations

**Pursue in this staged order:**

1. **First (0–3 months, cheap, high-defensibility): the "deflation ≠ leakage control" result + co-evolved risk red-team design (Candidate 3 core).** Reproduce Gençay's planted-oracle finding inside CELL//OS, then show that a co-evolved red-team Operative anchored to live-vs-backtest divergence catches leakage that DSR/PBO miss. This is publishable without a live deployment, plugs RQGM's named "anchor" limitation, and de-risks everything downstream. **CELL//OS components:** THE CRUCIBLE (adversarial eval), DEFLATOR (DSR/PBO), research firewall (structural sealing), JUDGE (degradation), DISTANCE (live anchor signal). **Benchmark that would change the plan:** if the red-team cannot beat a frozen DSR/PBO evaluator at predicting live divergence out-of-sample, drop the co-evolution framing and publish the negative "deflation insufficiency" result alone.

2. **Second (3–12 months, flagship: Deflated Gödel Machine, Candidate 1).** Only after the anchor and sealing are validated. Structure as RQGM-style monthly epochs over a 6–12 month shadow-trading window; acceptance gate = CPCV out-of-sample DSR (given pre-registered budget N) + PBO < 0.5 + embargoed live confirmation. Baselines must include PACE (e-process) and SEA (FWER/FDR) gates to honestly situate novelty. **CELL//OS components:** BLACKBOX (evolutionary discovery), hypothesis registry + backtest budget, DEFLATOR, PROPSIM, WARDEN (fail-closed), the Evolution Loop. **Threshold that would change the plan:** if accepted strategies' *live* Deflated Sharpe is indistinguishable from the "keep-if-Sharpe-up" baseline after the multiple-testing correction, the contribution collapses to the (still-valuable) methodological result — publish that and stop.

3. **Third (opportunistic: constraint-aware organizational evolution, Candidate 2), only if 1–2 show that information barriers measurably help.** Frame explicitly against ADAS/MaAS/GPTSwarm/AFlow with barriers/vetoes as *rewarded* search dimensions and clade-level credit assignment.

**Governance/publication strengtheners to include in every paper:** fail-closed constraints inside the self-improvement loop (WARDEN), human sign-off gates before promotion, full Neo4j lineage auditability, and pre-registration of the backtest budget. These are exactly the safety framings (sandboxing, human oversight) that DGM and AI-Scientist papers foreground, and reviewers now expect them.

## Caveats
- **Date/verification flags:** AQuA's cost, token budget, and iteration count are **not reported** in the paper (unverifiable). AQuA is **backtest-only** (verified absent live). Several finance evolutionary-agent arXiv IDs surfaced only via citations/snippets and their exact IDs should be double-checked before formal citation, though their characterization (no formal multiple-testing gate) is consistent across sources. QuantAgents' live returns are **self-reported and implausible** — do not rely on them. AFlow's ~$22.50 training figure circulated via the MaAS comparison; the AFlow paper's own headline economics are the 4.55%-of-GPT-4o inference-cost result cited above.
- **The novelty window is closing fast.** PACE (2606), SEA (2607), Gençay (2608), and AQuA (2608) all landed in mid-2026; the "acceptance-gate-as-multiple-testing" and "DSR-on-agentic-search" ideas are now claimed at the abstract level. The defensible novelty is specifically the *finance fusion + delayed live anchor + necessity-not-sufficiency* result, and it will erode if a live-anchored deflated self-improvement paper appears first.
- **Hype vs. established:** DGM/HGM/RQGM results are on *static coding benchmarks* and do not transfer automatically to noisy, non-stationary finance. "Groundbreaking" here means a rigorous methodological contribution, not a money-printing machine — any live-PnL claim must clear DSR/PBO/CPCV *and* structural leakage controls before publication.
- **Quantum framing:** keep it as an explicitly-labeled design metaphor. There is no empirical quantum-cognition-for-agents literature to anchor a scientific claim.
