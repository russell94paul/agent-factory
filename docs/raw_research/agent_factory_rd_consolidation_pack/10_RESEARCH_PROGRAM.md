## Research hypotheses

H1. Agent optimization beats a fixed hand-designed Agent.

H2. Team optimization beats independently optimized Agents.

H3. Knowledge/retrieval optimization improves Team performance further.

H4. Project-conditioned organization optimization beats one globally fixed Army.

H5. Joint optimization beats isolated component optimization.

H6. Bidirectional configuration search produces more useful optimization knowledge than forward-only search.

H7. Adaptive optimizer portfolios reduce experiments/cost versus one fixed optimizer.

H8. Forecast-aware curriculum improves future mission success compared with purely retrospective training.

H9. Mission-projected HyperMESH improves relevant-context precision and time-to-root-cause.

H10. Organization-level optimization can identify project-specific ephemeral organizations that outperform static team structures.
