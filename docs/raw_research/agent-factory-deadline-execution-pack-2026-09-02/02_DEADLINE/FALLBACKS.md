# Fallback Ladder

## A — target

Fresh Marketing mission runs end-to-end through Switchboard and produces the meeting artifact.

## B — acceptable

Switchboard creates/launches and performs most of the mission; a final deterministic or supervised step finishes the artifact. The UI still exposes evidence and run state.

## C — acceptable for the meeting

Switchboard demonstrates mission creation, selection, DAG/run behavior and artifact references, while the known-good validated Marketing artifact is used for the client review.

## D — delivery protection

Use the existing meeting-ready Marketing artifact independently of Agent Factory. Continue platform integration after the meeting.

Never hide which fallback was used.
