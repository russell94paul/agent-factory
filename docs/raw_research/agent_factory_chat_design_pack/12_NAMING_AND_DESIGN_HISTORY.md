# 12 — Naming & Design History

## Stage 1 — Agentic IDE
The discussion began with the need for an interface that is not merely an agent dashboard, but a new engineering paradigm for supervising artificial organizations.

Status: **CURRENT / CORE**

## Stage 2 — Army Base / Organizational World
A second animated world was proposed for presence, communication, social features, and visual organizational activity.

Status: **CURRENT as alternate UI**

## Stage 3 — Autonomous Lab
A cross-team autonomous lab was proposed to ingest company experience, failures, successes, ideas, research, product signals, and customer evidence and find the highest-leverage changes.

Status: **CURRENT concept, renamed Advanced Projects Command**

## Stage 4 — Zeus / Greek Mythology Naming
The entire estate was explored through Zeus/Olympus mythology.

Useful lessons:
- consistent world vocabulary matters,
- system metaphors can improve architecture memorability,
- distinct knowledge/memory/communication/governance roles benefit from thematic identity.

Status: **SUPERSEDED for UI theme**

## Stage 5 — Full Army Rebrand
The user requested extreme Army / mission-operations theming.

Current world vocabulary:

Status: **CURRENT**

## Stage 6 — Configurable Service Designations
The hierarchy was extended from a few fixed levels to arbitrarily deep configurable organizational trees.

Status: **CORE**

## Stage 7 — Recurring Failure Rate
Recurring failure became a first-class health/learning metric, not just raw failure count.

Status: **CORE**
