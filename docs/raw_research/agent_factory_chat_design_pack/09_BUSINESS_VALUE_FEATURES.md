# 09 — 10 Business-Value-First Concepts

## 1. Surveillance / Satellite Mode
Show only incidents, blockers, queues, abnormal spend, uncertainty, unanswered distress, and approvals.

Replaces:
multiple status/alert/approval dashboards.

Business hypothesis:
reduce time-to-understand and operator attention cost.

## 2. Reinforcement Router
Find the best available human/agent/team based on expertise, prior similar missions, availability, cost, and permissions.

Replaces:
manual directory search + Slack “who knows X?” + context handoff.

Business hypothesis:
reduce blocker duration and coordination overhead.

## 3. Collision Control
Detect duplicate work, overlapping file changes, contradictory assumptions, or scarce-resource conflict.

Replaces:
late merge-conflict discovery and duplicate investigation.

Business hypothesis:
reduce wasted agent cost and rework.

## 4. Command Authorization
Unified governed approval surface for consequential actions.

Replaces:
scattered approvals across GitHub, workflows, chat, deployment tools.

Business hypothesis:
reduce approval latency while improving auditability.

## 5. Frontline Optimizer
Prioritize missions against explicit Commander's Intent, dependencies, risk, urgency, and business value.

Replaces:
priority labels, roadmap sorting, repeated reprioritization meetings.

Business hypothesis:
increase value delivered per unit time.

## 6. Logistics Health
Continuously assess whether a mission has required context, credentials, tools, compute, environment, dependencies, and budget.

Replaces:
manual preflight and multiple diagnostic pages.

Business hypothesis:
increase first-pass mission success and reduce avoidable runtime failure.

## 7. After-Action Learning
Automatically structure outcome, successful tactics, failures, reusable lessons, new threats, and candidate doctrine.

Replaces:
manual retro/postmortem/knowledge capture.

Business hypothesis:
increase knowledge reuse and reduce recurrence.

## 8. Workflow Compression
Mine repeated stable agent/tool sequences and turn them into deterministic skills/meta-tools.

Replaces:
repeated expensive reasoning.

Business hypothesis:
reduce tokens, latency, variance, and failure surface.

## 9. Advanced Projects Command
Continuously discover and validate the highest-leverage organizational/product/process improvements.

Replaces:
fragmented innovation backlog and ad-hoc R&D.

Business hypothesis:
improve rate of validated high-value change.

## 10. Objective Ledger / Commander's Intent
Connect each operation to explicit success criteria and show actual contribution.

Replaces:
disconnected OKRs, roadmaps, project status, and engineering activity metrics.

Business hypothesis:
reduce activity optimization and improve outcome alignment.
