# 13 — Ready-to-Run Research Prompts

## Prompt A — Agentic IDE Interaction Model

You are researching a new class of software interface: an Agentic IDE for commanding artificial organizations rather than editing files or chatting with one agent.

Research the best interaction primitives for one engineer supervising many agent teams. Compare modern coding-agent UIs, workflow orchestrators, distributed-systems observability, human-swarm interfaces, ecological interface design, RTS command interfaces, mission-control systems, process mining, collaborative IDEs, organizational theory, and multi-agent HCI.

Focus on:
1. macro vs micro command,
2. attention scalability,
3. mixed-initiative control,
4. group/formation operations,
5. intervention and approvals,
6. causal replay,
7. uncertainty representation,
8. context/logistics,
9. collision detection,
10. outcome-oriented coordination.

For every concept:
- name,
- interaction,
- what traditional UI it replaces,
- measurable benefit,
- risks,
- backend requirements,
- recommended evaluation,
- keep/kill criteria.

Do not assume gamification is beneficial. Identify where dense conventional UI is superior.

## Prompt F — Gamification Without Goodhart

Research gamification for engineering organizations with humans and AI agents.

Goal:
increase useful ideas, knowledge contribution, cross-team communication, experimentation, and social presence.

Avoid:
message-count rewards, task-count optimization, agent-count optimization, unsafe speed, popularity contests, performative activity.

Study:
remote-company social worlds, multiplayer collaborative environments, game UX, organizational psychology, intrinsic motivation, public/private recognition, social presence, Goodhart’s Law.

Design:
- ranks,
- certifications,
- callsigns,
- quests,
- cross-team combo recognition,
- failed-experiment celebration,
- learning contribution,
- world events,
- social micro-interactions.

For every mechanic specify:
desired behaviour, possible gaming behaviour, countermeasure, privacy implications, and measurement.
