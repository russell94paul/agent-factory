# Novelty Claims and Research Boundaries

## Important stance

Do not claim that the individual mechanisms are new.

Most have substantial prior art:

- mixture-of-experts,
- online learning with expert advice,
- weighted majority,
- dynamic ensembles,
- routing,
- causal inference,
- conformal prediction,
- RAG,
- knowledge graphs,
- debate/critics,
- digital twins,
- active learning,
- continual learning,
- model predictive control,
- CVaR,
- transactive memory,
- stigmergy,
- markets,
- organizational cybernetics,
- evolutionary algorithms.

The stronger possible novelty boundary is the integrated architecture and its recursive application.

## Defensible candidate contribution

A possible future research claim:

> A persistent computational entity whose cognitive topology, expert trust, evidence acquisition, verification effort, risk posture, authority, memory, health, and configuration are jointly governed as adaptive state variables, with the same orchestration principles recursively composable across expert, agent, team, and organizational levels.

This must still be tested against prior art.

## Candidate differentiators to validate

1. **Mutable cognition** — reasoning topology itself changes at runtime.
2. **Recursive SIHRE** — routing/trust/uncertainty applies from internal experts through organizational levels.
3. **Cognitive portfolio optimization** — error covariance becomes a first-class team/expert selection variable.
4. **Contextual trust** — evidence-based trust conditioned on mission state, not a global reputation number.
5. **Epistemic metabolism** — resources are explicitly traded for information gain / expected utility improvement.
6. **Predictive cognitive immunity** — failure memories alter cognition before recurrence.
7. **Homeostatic cognition** — cognitive operating state is controlled, not merely observed.
8. **Earned autonomy** — authority is an empirical and risk-conditioned variable.
9. **Developmental agents** — specialization and rank are discovered from evidence.
10. **Cognitive genome + lineage** — architecture itself becomes the object of controlled experimentation.

## Claims to avoid without evidence

Avoid:

- "first self-improving agent",
- "new form of intelligence",
- "self-aware agent",
- "human-like consciousness",
- "world's first recursive agent",
- "guaranteed safer",
- "dramatically better performance",
- "novel because it uses multiple experts".

Prefer:

- proposed,
- architecture,
- research hypothesis,
- intended to,
- empirically test,
- integrates,
- recursive application,
- context-conditioned governance,
- system-level contribution.

## Required evidence for stronger claims

- temporal holdout evaluation,
- ablation tests,
- private-repo mission benchmarks,
- statistically significant gains,
- calibration curves,
- tail-risk analysis,
- failure-correlation analysis,
- cost/latency tradeoffs,
- evidence that dynamic routing beats fixed routing,
- evidence that contextual trust predicts success,
- evidence that new complexity does not simply shift failures elsewhere.
