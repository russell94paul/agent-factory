# DR08 — What Comes After the Standard AI Agent?

Investigate whether the proposed architecture is conceptually distinct enough to justify a new entity term.

Candidate characteristics:
- persistent identity and evidence history,
- self-model/digital twin,
- mutable reasoning topology,
- heterogeneous cognitive portfolio,
- contextual trust,
- uncertainty-aware abstention,
- active evidence gathering,
- homeostasis,
- predictive immunity,
- developmental specialization,
- dynamic authority,
- evolvable cognitive genome,
- recursive organizational composition.

Compare against:
- AI agent,
- autonomous agent,
- cognitive architecture,
- compound AI system,
- digital organism,
- autonomic system,
- multi-agent system,
- actor,
- cybernetic system,
- viable system,
- artificial life,
- software organism.

Output:
- taxonomy,
- minimum defining properties,
- terminology collisions,
- naming options,
- academic-safe term,
- product-friendly term,
- what would justify a new term versus simply "advanced agent."
