# DR07 — Cross-Domain Mechanism Mining for Agent 2.0

Search for transferable mechanisms in:

- human cognition,
- neuroscience,
- immunology,
- physiology,
- ecology,
- swarm systems,
- evolutionary biology,
- military command,
- aviation,
- emergency response,
- manufacturing,
- logistics,
- SRE,
- high-reliability organizations,
- markets,
- portfolio management,
- sports teams,
- education/apprenticeship,
- organizational design,
- cybernetics.

Find at least 40 candidate mechanisms.

For each:
1. Original domain.
2. Mechanism.
3. Why it works there.
4. Precise computational analogue.
5. What would be superficial anthropomorphism.
6. Agent Factory feature.
7. Expected benefit.
8. Failure mode.
9. Measurement.
10. Small falsifiable experiment.
11. Prior implementation in AI/software.
12. Novelty potential of the transfer.

Rank the top 15 by expected practical value.
