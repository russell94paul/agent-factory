# CELL OS — narrator script

**90.0 s master · 156 words · 20 lines · target read 2.4 w/s · 65.0 s of speech, 25.0 s of silence**

Timecodes are the **in-points** and they are authoritative — the picture cuts to them, not the
reverse.

---

## Direction for the read

Not an advertisement. The register is a **computing-architecture announcement** — someone who
already knows this matters and is not going to raise their voice about it. The nearest references are
the opening five minutes of a technical keynote, or the narration of an aerospace programme film.

- **Down at the end of every sentence.** Every line lands; none reaches.
- **Full stops are real stops.** The marked rests are the performance, not the gaps between it. A
  read that fills them is the wrong read even if every word is correct.
- **No smile in the voice, and no warmth added at the end.** The last spoken line is not a sign-off.
- **Technical nouns get more time, never more volume** — *Operative*, *Cell*, *kernel*, *evidence*,
  *instrument*. Weight by duration.
- Narrow dynamic range, close mic, minimal room. Slight proximity, no breathiness.
- **Two lines are the whole film** — L15 and L16, marked ⭐. Everything else supports them.

⛔ Do not "sell" L16. It is the most important sentence in the script and it must arrive as a plain
statement of fact from someone slightly bored of having to repeat it.

|---|---|
| CELL OS | *"cell oh-ess."* Two words, a small gap between them. ⛔ Never glided into *"cellos"* |
| Operative | **OP**-er-a-tiv. Stress the first syllable. Not *op-ER-ative* |
| HyperMESH | ⛔ not spoken anywhere in this cut. On screen only |
| Org-IR | not spoken. On screen only |
| UNMEASURABLE | ⛔ **never spoken.** It is only ever read. L16 describes it without naming it, and that is why the word on screen carries weight |
| L03 | comma-break after *"memory"* so four attributes land as four |
| L10 | no lift on *"uncertainty"* — it is a condition, not a drama |
| L16 | *"could not run"* is one unit, one breath, no comma pause. Then a hard stop before *"has not passed"* |
| L20 | ⭐ *"could"* must be audible. It is the modal that makes the sentence true — `claim-ledger.md` C15 |

## Modal discipline — the compliance check

Every line was written against `claim-ledger.md`. The modals are not stylistic:

| Line | Says | Not | Because |
|---|---|---|---|
| L06 | *is built for* | *manages* | C3 — no runtime exists |
| L08 | *is designed to compile* | *compiles* | C4 — Org-IR is Specified, never built, and the category is contested |
| L10 | *can expand* | *expands* | C5 — the one formation we built was rejected on evidence |
| L11 | *is designed to work like* | *treats* | C6 — HyperMESH is the corpus's most contested concept |
| L14 | *can pass* | *passes* / *blocks* | C7 — the preflight is WARN-ONLY; it refuses nothing |
| L15 | *is not* | — | C9 — **MEASURED.** Indicative earned |
| L16 | *has not passed* | — | C10 — **MEASURED.** Indicative earned |
| L20 | *could direct* | *directs* | C15 — zero real runs exist |

⭐ Two of twenty lines speak in the present indicative, and both are true. That ratio is the film's
integrity, and an editor who "tightens" a modal out of a line breaks it.

## Casting

- **Range** — low-mid. No gravel affectation. Neither the movie-trailer register nor the
  founder-keynote register.
- **Accent** — neutral international, mild. Regional colour is fine; performed authority is not.
- **Gender** — unspecified deliberately. Cast on restraint, not timbre.
- ⭐ **Worth auditioning:** a voice that sounds like an *engineer* rather than a narrator. Slight
  imperfection buys more credibility here than polish does, and 156 words is short enough to survive
  a non-professional read.

⚠ Record L16 at least six times.

## Synthetic-read fallback (previz only)

Rate **0.86×**, pitch neutral, and insert every marked rest as an explicit break. Most engines
collapse unmarked pauses, which would run the film ~9 s short with no silence — precisely the failure
this script is built to prevent. The master gets a human.
