What the arithmetic decided

Four numbers settled the cut before a word of script was written. Each is computed, not chosen — the working is in timing.md.

The brief's own conflict

118s

The brief asks for 75–90 seconds and its section timecodes sum to 118. Its 234 words of narration need 101.7s of speech alone, before a single pause. It cannot be shot as written inside its own runtime.

Word budget vs delivered

156 / 163

(90.0 − 16.0) × 2.4 w/s, less 8% for pauses, gives 163 words. Delivered 156 — a 32.9% compression of the brief, with 7 words spare.

Silence, as a budgeted quantity

25.0s

65.0s of speech in a 90.0s film. The silence is designed and allocated shot by shot, which is why the density ceiling holds where eight labels land at once.

Claims in the present indicative

2 of 20

Two narration lines speak in the indicative, and both are measured in this repository. The other eighteen are modal — can, is designed to, could. That ratio is the film's integrity.

The anchor

One reel in this film is built, tested, validated and standards-grounded. It is shot 23, it gets the most screen time of any reel, and its content was already better than anything invented for it.

factory/contract.py · five verdicts, never collapsed

NOT_RUN < PASS < UNMEASURABLE < FAIL < ERROR

“A check whose instrument could not run has not passed.”

Grounded in ISO/IEC 9646 and carried still by TTCN-3 (ITU-T Z.140 §24.2). Every competitor can claim orchestration; none ships a verdict meaning the thing that was supposed to measure this could not see. The brief's invented VERIFIED_SUCCESS returns zero hits in the repository and was cut in its favour.
