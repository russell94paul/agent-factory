Where this argument breaks

Lanes are capped at three. The conflict graph in factory/lanes.py puts control-plane against judgement and certify against grain — both pairs edit one file. Four agents cannot all run. Raise the agent slider and the lanes model simply leaves one idle.

So at a low enough conflict rate the shared checkout wins, and the bench will say so. That is not a flaw in the demo. A comparison that cannot lose is a brochure.

The competitor columns are models, not products. We have measured numbers for our own runs and none for anyone else's, so naming a vendor and asserting its throughput would be inventing evidence. Every figure attached to solo and shared is DERIVED from the one conflict rate we did measure.

The 41.7% default is the measured cross-agent conflict rate on a shared branch. Everything downstream of it moves when you move it.
