What you asked for

Our current understanding of the outcome you asked us to deliver. If any of this is wrong, it is the most valuable thing you can correct today.

Establish one agreed, evidence-backed definition of the Navira cross-channel marketing model — what it must answer, at what grain, from which data — before any further build work is committed to it.

Acceptance criteria

  • Requirements are separated into what you confirmed, what we inferred, and what is unresolved.
  • Each candidate design declares its grain and is tested against the questions it must answer.
  • A named human signs off the recommendation before any build work follows from it.

Requirements

  • Read-only against your systems. No client state is modified at any point in this phase.
  • Every requirement traced to who actually said it — you, or us on your behalf.
  • The design must state its grain explicitly before any measure is designed.
  • An absence in the data must never render as a zero.

Assumptions we are working under

  • Navira (HOUSE) is the entity the target-state model is being designed for.
  • Work targets the TEST environment; production is not read or written in this phase.

Explicitly out of scope

  • Lectric is not part of the target-state model. It exists in the estate today and appears in the historical record, and this phase does not remove it — it excludes it from the model being designed. Its historical values are therefore not carried forward as part of the future model, and they should not be read as if they were.
  • The known defect in Lectric's metric path is not a repair requirement for the target-state model, and no fix for it is proposed or costed here. It is recorded as a property of the current estate, not as work this design owes you.
  • No changes to the existing Power BI model or the marketing dashboard during reconstruction.
  • No production deployment in this phase.

Still unresolved

  • The boundary between daily reporting metrics and marketing metrics rests on a recollection of a request, not on a record of one. Reading the meeting transcript did not support it. This needs your confirmation — see Decisions.
  • Ten referenced tickets and several email threads are not readable from our repositories, so any requirement resting only on those is recorded as reported-to-us, never as confirmed.
  • Taking Lectric out of the target-state model is a scope decision, not a change to your live systems. Nothing has been removed. The order in which it could safely be removed, and which existing consumers would be left pointing at nothing if it were, have not been verified — that work is not part of this phase and is not being claimed as done.

Do you want the six remaining divergent warehouse objects repointed now, or as part of the model rebuild?

Our recommendation. Repoint them as part of the model rebuild, so the change is made once against an agreed design rather than twice.
