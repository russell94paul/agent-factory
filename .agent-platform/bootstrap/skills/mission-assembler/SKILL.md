# Mission Assembler

This is initially a deterministic/design skill, not an autonomous topology optimizer.

1. Classify mission family, risk and temporal class.
2. Identify required capabilities and authority.
3. Retrieve prior similar missions/experts.
4. Inspect available agents/skills/tools/compute.
5. Choose a tested blueprint or smallest adequate composition.
6. Decide owner vs parallel workers vs consulted experts.
7. Emit communication subscriptions/routes.
8. Emit role-specific context packet requirements.
9. Attach budget, permissions and GreenContract.
10. Produce a resolved Mission Assembly Plan and reason for every participant.

Reject participants with no clear contribution.
