# Revenue & Autonomous Venture Flywheel

## Thesis

Agent Factory can evolve from a system that builds software into a system that repeatedly **researches opportunities, assembles the right organization, builds software, observes market response, improves the product, and reuses the resulting organizational knowledge**.

The credible near-term target is **low-human software businesses**, not a claim that all businesses can or should operate with zero humans.

Humans remain appropriate at consequential gates such as legal commitments, financial authority, regulated claims, security-sensitive changes, major public positioning, destructive production actions and strategic capital allocation until evidence supports narrower delegation.

## Commercial evidence ladder

Do not label a venture successful because the software shipped.

```text
IDEA
  ↓
problem evidence
  ↓
segment evidence
  ↓
willingness-to-pay evidence
  ↓
acquisition evidence
  ↓
activation evidence
  ↓
retention evidence
  ↓
positive unit-economics evidence
  ↓
repeatable operating evidence
  ↓
scalable / durable business evidence
```

Different products may skip or redefine individual measures, but the core rule remains: **shipping is activity; a business outcome requires external evidence.**

## Candidate organizations

### Monetization Research Council

Roles/capabilities may include:

- market scout;
- problem miner;
- competitor/prior-art analyst;
- customer-segment researcher;
- pricing strategist;
- distribution researcher;
- skeptic/falsifier;
- synthesis/evidence curator.

### Venture Build Pod

- product spec/UX;
- architecture;
- implementation;
- deterministic test/eval stages;
- security/reliability review;
- launch artifact preparation.

### Growth & Learning Pod

- product analytics;
- onboarding experimenter;
- content/SEO research where appropriate;
- support synthesis;
- retention/churn analyst;
- experiment designer.

Growth automation must obey platform policies. Do not use deceptive claims, impersonation, uncontrolled mass outreach, fake reviews, dark patterns or spam as "autonomous growth."

### Venture Reliability Corps

- service health;
- incident triage;
- cost drift;
- data quality;
- support escalation;
- maintenance missions.

## The compounding asset

The most valuable output of each venture is not only code or revenue. It is a growing library of evidence-backed reusable capability:

```text
successful mission
  → reusable experience
  → improved skill
  → improved team blueprint
  → deterministic meta-tool candidate
  → stronger eval corpus
  → better mission assembly
  → cheaper / faster / more reliable next venture
```

This is where the platform can become meaningfully different from a simple coding-agent orchestrator.

## Human authority boundaries

Default human/policy gates should remain for:

- opening or materially changing financial accounts;
- unrestricted spending or payment authority;
- signing contracts or accepting legal terms beyond predefined policy;
- regulated-domain decisions;
- publishing consequential claims that have not passed policy/evidence review;
- handling sensitive customer data outside declared permissions;
- major production deletion/migration;
- acquiring a company or incurring debt;
- changing the evaluator/promotion rules governing self-improvement.

The goal is to eliminate repetitive coordination, not eliminate accountable ownership.
