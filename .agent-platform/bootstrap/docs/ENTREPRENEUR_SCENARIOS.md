# Entrepreneur / Career Scenarios Enabled by the Platform

These are scenario designs, not promises of automatic profitability. The purpose is to identify product capabilities that can be tested against real outcomes.

## Scenario 1 — The One-Person Micro-SaaS Studio

A founder defines a domain and budget. The platform runs opportunity research, ranks narrow problems, validates demand, assembles a product pod, builds a tiny subscription product, launches a controlled experiment, and routes customer feedback back into the product team.

The founder mainly handles judgment gates such as whether to fund the next experiment, public positioning and consequential commercial/legal choices.

Compounding asset: every failed and successful launch improves the opportunity, build and onboarding playbooks.

## Scenario 2 — Autonomous Internal Tools Company

Instead of selling one SaaS product, the system continuously discovers repetitive workflows inside small businesses and creates narrowly scoped tools or managed agent workflows.

Potential model:

```text
business pain discovery
→ workflow evidence
→ fixed-scope automation
→ managed monthly service
→ reusable vertical capability
```

One successful capability can become a repeatable product across similar customers.

## Scenario 3 — Agent Team as a Product

A particularly strong software team configuration becomes a certified asset.

Customers do not buy a generic chatbot. They subscribe to a proven organization such as:

- migration team;
- API integration pod;
- data-quality guardian;
- reliability team;
- research council;
- UI/product studio.

The differentiator is the evidence package: supported mission family, evaluation history, cost, reliability, tools, permissions and known limits.

## Scenario 4 — Continuous Product Resurrection

The platform receives a dormant/underperforming codebase and runs a recurring cycle:

```text
repo + product telemetry + customer complaints
→ diagnose highest-value bottleneck
→ research alternatives
→ build candidate improvements
→ test/canary
→ measure activation/retention/support impact
→ keep or roll back
```

A founder could operate a portfolio of small products that receive bounded continuous improvement rather than requiring full-time manual product teams.

## Scenario 5 — The Software Portfolio Laboratory

Instead of betting the entire budget on one idea, the system researches ten opportunities, cheaply validates five, prototypes three, launches two, and scales only the one that earns external evidence.

Agent teams, prompts, context strategies and product patterns that work are promoted into the shared capability registry.

This is where software construction and organizational evolution meet portfolio management.

## Scenario 6 — Industry-Specific Autonomous Operations

The platform is configured for one narrow domain where recurring workflows, APIs and business rules are well understood. It builds and operates subscription software around those workflows while a human remains responsible for contracts, regulated decisions and high-consequence changes.

The commercial advantage is cumulative domain cognition: each customer teaches the organization new edge cases, integrations and successful operating patterns within permission boundaries.

## Scenario 7 — Entrepreneur Career Shift

A technically strong founder gradually shifts from:

```text
writing every implementation
→ directing agents
→ designing organizations
→ selecting experiments
→ allocating capital/time
→ governing a portfolio of software organizations
```

The software becomes leverage for the founder's judgment rather than an attempt to remove judgment entirely.
