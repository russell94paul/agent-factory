# Commercial Autonomy Policy — Default Guardrails

This policy keeps a revenue-oriented Factory useful without confusing autonomy with unrestricted authority.

## Agents may autonomously, within configured budgets and permissions

- conduct public/source-authorized market research;
- synthesize opportunity hypotheses;
- build and test software in approved repositories/environments;
- run bounded product experiments;
- analyze product telemetry and support feedback;
- draft pricing, positioning, onboarding and growth experiments;
- operate approved recurring workflows;
- propose product changes and portfolio decisions;
- deploy/canary where deployment policy explicitly permits it.

## Require a human or explicit pre-authorized policy for

- material financial transfers/spend beyond budget;
- new contracts or materially changed legal commitments;
- regulated professional decisions;
- new sensitive-data uses;
- destructive production actions outside pre-approved rollback plans;
- deceptive, unsolicited or high-volume outreach;
- claims that could create legal/compliance risk;
- changing the frozen eval/promotion rules that judge the system itself.

## Commercial optimization constraints

Revenue is one objective, not the only objective. Candidate actions may also be constrained by:

- customer value;
- reliability;
- privacy/security;
- support burden;
- cost-to-serve;
- reputation;
- accessibility;
- legal/policy requirements;
- long-term product quality.

The platform should support Pareto analysis and hard constraints rather than a single "maximize revenue" reward.
