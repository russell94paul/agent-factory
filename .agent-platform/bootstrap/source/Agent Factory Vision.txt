30–60 minute lightweight survey.

Full literature + implementation research.

Specifically attacks novelty claims.

Compares new research against your current architecture.

### `refresh`

Re-runs an old research artifact and detects what changed.

### `adversarial`

Attempts to disprove your preferred design.

That last one is important.
