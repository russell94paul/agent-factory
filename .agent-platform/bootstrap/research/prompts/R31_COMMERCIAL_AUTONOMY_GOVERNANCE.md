# R31 — Commercial Autonomy, Venture Governance & Economic Control

## Objective

Determine how Agent Factory can support low-human software ventures while maintaining explicit financial, legal, security, customer-data and production authority boundaries.

## Questions

1. Which commercial actions are safely automatable under fixed budgets/policies?
2. Which actions should require approval because they create legal, financial, privacy or reputational commitments?
3. How should venture budgets, cloud/model spend, refunds, pricing experiments and acquisition spend be represented?
4. How should a venture prove willingness-to-pay, activation, retention and unit economics before scaling?
5. What controls prevent agents from optimizing for spam, deceptive growth, fake engagement, or misleading claims?
6. How should the platform separate research recommendations from authority to deploy/spend/publish?
7. How should customer-impacting changes use canaries, feature flags and rollback?
8. What evidence is required before increasing an organization's autonomy tier?

## Required outputs

- commercial authority matrix;
- autonomy tiers;
- venture budget contract;
- launch/publish/payment gate design;
- safe growth-policy baseline;
- unit-economics evidence contract;
- incident/rollback model;
- recommendations for the first bounded commercial pilot.
