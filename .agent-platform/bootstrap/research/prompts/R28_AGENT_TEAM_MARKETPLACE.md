# R28 — Certified Agent/Team Capability Market & Business Models

## Objective

Research whether proven agent/skill/team configurations can become reusable commercial products and what evidence, packaging, security and business model would make such a market trustworthy.

## Questions

- What exactly is sold: prompt, skill, agent bundle, team blueprint, managed workflow, outcome, or hosted organization?
- How do buyers evaluate capability without relying on benchmark theater?
- How should task-family scope, eval history, costs, permissions, model dependencies and regression risk be represented?
- How does versioning/re-certification work when models/providers change?
- Which marketplace/business models resemble this concept, and what lessons transfer?
- What prevents unsafe or malicious skills/tools from entering the ecosystem?
- How can private customer evals be used without leaking data?
- When should the platform sell software produced by teams vs sell the teams/workflows themselves?

## Outputs

- product taxonomy;
- capability-certification model;
- trust/reputation architecture;
- candidate pricing/business models;
- marketplace security model;
- MVP experiment;
- reasons not to build a marketplace yet.
