# R26 — Autonomous Monetization & Opportunity Intelligence

## Objective

Research the best evidence-based architecture for an agent organization that continuously discovers, evaluates and prioritizes monetizable software opportunities without confusing automated research with proven demand.

## Core questions

1. Which market/customer signals are reliable enough to generate software opportunity hypotheses?
2. How should multiple research agents divide competitor, customer, pricing, distribution, operational and prior-art research?
3. How should contradictory evidence and source quality affect confidence?
4. What low-cost experiments best test willingness to pay before substantial implementation?
5. Which parts can be automated safely and which require human/customer contact or policy gates?
6. How should findings enter Collective Cognition without polluting it with speculative market claims?
7. How should an OpportunityHypothesis be versioned and falsified?
8. What failure modes occur in autonomous market research: confirmation bias, fake demand, SEO noise, stale pricing, survivorship bias, fabricated personas, copied ideas?

## Required research

Compare academic/practitioner evidence on customer discovery, lean experimentation, pricing research, prediction/forecasting, automated market intelligence, multi-agent research, product analytics and software venture studios. Include working agentic/open-source implementations where relevant.

Assume the preferred architecture may be unnecessarily complicated. Identify the simplest workflow that could outperform one strong researcher using the same sources.

## Required outputs

- prior-art matrix;
- signal taxonomy;
- opportunity-hypothesis schema critique;
- automation vs human boundary;
- recommended research organization;
- falsification experiments;
- architecture impact on Agent Factory;
- risks and anti-Goodhart controls;
- implementation experiments suitable for the current Factory.

Label claims: `MEASURED EVIDENCE | DOCUMENTED PRACTICE | INFERENCE | HYPOTHESIS | SPECULATION`.
